How The Audit Works

The system is built around a multi-agent research process designed to challenge the investment case from multiple angles.

I. Automated Microeconomic Audits

Target assets are analyzed through a cluster of AI analyst agents that map operational quality, cost structure, asset footprint, customer behavior, and qualitative moat indicators.

II. The Anti-Moat Friction Panel

To reduce confirmation bias, dedicated adversarial agents stress-test the company against historical failure patterns, disruption risks, and weakening competitive advantages.

III. Multi-Tier Investment Synthesis

The system synthesizes competing arguments into a structured report focused on business quality, durability, management incentives, valuation risk, and cognitive bias.
